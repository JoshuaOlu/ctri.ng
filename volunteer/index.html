Looking for Something More Sustained?

Consider the CFK Fellowship.

The CFK Fellowship is a structured volunteer community for young Nigerians making long-term contributions to CFK's work — including CTRI. Fellows receive progression, recognition, and a genuine community of people committed to the same mission. If you want to go deeper than a single volunteer role, the Fellowship is where that happens.
