Why CTRI Exists

Three barriers stand between Nigerian students and the resources they need.

💰
The cost barrier

Quality textbooks exist. Many Nigerian students simply cannot afford them. The solution is not to write better textbooks — it is to write free ones.

🌍
The context barrier

Free resources exist — but most were written outside Nigeria. A student who cannot locate themselves in a textbook still faces a barrier. Context is not cosmetic. It is part of how learning works.

📖
The register barrier

Even good textbooks are often written in formal academic language that is itself a barrier. CTRI writes like a teacher explaining something — not a textbook performing complexity.

How It Works

Built openly. Improved continuously.

01
Write

University-level contributors write chapters in conversational, teacher-like language — structured for self-study, specific to the Nigerian context.

02
Review

Chapters are reviewed by subject experts and tested with actual secondary school students. Feedback shapes every revision.

03
Publish

Completed chapters are published as open educational resources — free to access online, free to download, free to print.

04
Distribute

Because CFK owns the copyright, printed copies can reach students in hard-to-reach communities where internet access is limited.
