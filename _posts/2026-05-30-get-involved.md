## Join CTRI

The Collaborative Tutorial Resources Initiative (CTRI) is a collaborative educational project focused on building clear, structured SSCE textbooks for secondary school students.

We are currently inviting volunteers and contributors to join the initiative.

---

## Who We Are Looking For

CTRI is open to individuals who want to contribute in the following areas:

### 1. Content Contributors
- Subject matter writers
- Educators and tutors
- Students with strong subject understanding

### 2. Reviewers
- Teachers
- Academic reviewers
- Individuals who can check clarity and accuracy

### 3. Designers
- Diagram creators
- Educational illustrators
- Layout designers for learning materials

### 4. General Volunteers
- People interested in education reform
- Students willing to provide feedback on materials
- Individuals passionate about learning access

---

## What You Will Work On

Contributors will help in building:

- SSCE textbooks
- Chapter outlines
- Learning explanations
- Practice materials
- Visual diagrams and illustrations

All work is part of a structured, collaborative process.

---

## Why Join CTRI?

CTRI is more than a content project. It is a long-term effort to improve access to quality educational materials.

By contributing, you will:

- Support students preparing for SSCE
- Contribute to open educational resources
- Be part of a structured textbook development system
- Help improve education accessibility in Nigeria

---

## How Contribution Works

CTRI follows a structured workflow:

1. Topic assignment or selection  
2. Content drafting  
3. Review and feedback  
4. Revision  
5. Final version release  

Each contribution is tracked and credited appropriately.
