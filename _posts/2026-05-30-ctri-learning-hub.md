## Welcome to the CTRI Learning Hub

The **Collaborative Tutorial Resources Initiative (CTRI)** is building a free, openly available textbook series for Nigerian secondary school students preparing for SSCE examinations — covering WAEC, JAMB and NECO.

Every textbook in the series is:
- Aligned to the official WAEC/JAMB/NECO syllabus
- Written for self-study, without teacher dependency
- Organised into logical themes and chapters
- Released progressively as chapters are completed
- Free to access and download

This page is your starting point. Use the links below to navigate to the subject hub of your choice.

## How Development Works

CTRI uses a living-document approach. Every chapter moves through the following stages:

| Stage           | Version   |
| --------------- | --------- |
| Draft           | 0.1       |
| Under Review    | 0.5       |
| Published       | 1.0       |
| Revised Edition | 1.1, 1.2… |

Chapters are released one at a time as they are completed and reviewed. The subject hub pages are updated with each release.
