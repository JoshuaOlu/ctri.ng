## CTRI Roadmap Overview

The Collaborative Tutorial Resources Initiative (CTRI) is working toward a clear long-term goal:

> To develop 10 high-quality SSCE textbooks by the year 2030.

This roadmap outlines the subjects, progress, and structure of the textbooks currently under development.

---

## Our Current Focus Subjects

CTRI is currently developing textbooks in the following areas:

- Mathematics  
- Physics  
- Chemistry  
- Biology  
- Agricultural Science  

These subjects form the foundation of the initial phase of CTRI’s textbook development.

---

## Development Structure

Each CTRI textbook is built using a structured approach:

- Clear conceptual progression  
- Step-by-step explanations  
- Self-study-friendly language  
- Continuous revision based on feedback  

Each subject is divided into chapters that evolve through versions:

- Draft (0.1)
- Review (0.5)
- Version 1.0 (Published)
- Updated Editions (1.1, 1.2, etc.)

## Version Philosophy

CTRI uses a living-document approach:

- Textbooks are not static
- Chapters improve over time
- Feedback from students is actively used
- Updates are tracked transparently

This ensures that learning materials remain relevant and effective.

---

## How We Will Reach 10 Textbooks

CTRI will scale gradually:

### Phase 1
- Core science subjects (current focus)
- Establish structure and feedback system

### Phase 2
- Expansion to additional SSCE subjects
- Contributor network growth

### Phase 3
- Full textbook completion (10 subjects total)
- Refinement and nationwide accessibility
