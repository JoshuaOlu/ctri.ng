## SSCE Physics (CTRI Learning Hub)

This page serves as the central hub for the development of the SSCE Physics textbook under the Collaborative Tutorial Resources Initiative (CTRI). It contains the roadmap, chapter structure, and updates for all Physics learning materials.

---

## About This Textbook

The CTRI Physics textbook is being designed to support self-study learning for secondary school students preparing for SSCE examinations (WAEC, JAMB & NECO).

The goal is to make Physics:
- Clear and understandable
- Structured in logical progression
- Supported with step-by-step explanations
- Accessible without teacher dependency

---

## Current Development Status

The Physics textbook is currently in early development. We are actively working on structuring the first set of chapters.

---

## Physics Textbook Roadmap

The textbook is organised into **six pedagogical Themes**, each functioning as a mini-book. Chapters are numbered within each Theme.

*The language of Physics — how we measure and describe the physical world*

| Chapter | Title                 |
| ------- | --------------------- |
| 1.1     | Measurement and Units |
| 1.2     | Properties of Matter  |

| ------- | ------------------------------- |
| 2.1     | Types of Motion and Forces      |
| 2.2     | Linear Motion and Graphs        |
| 2.3     | Newton's Laws of Motion         |
| 2.4     | Equilibrium and Circular Motion |
