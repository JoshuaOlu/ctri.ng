## Introducing CTRI

The Collaborative Tutorial Resources Initiative (CTRI) is an educational initiative focused on developing clear, self-study-friendly textbooks for SSCE students.

CTRI is an initiative of Care for Knowledge, committed to improving access to high-quality learning resources for secondary school education.

👉 https://careforknowledge.org

---

## Our Mission

We are building structured, easy-to-understand textbooks that help students learn independently without depending solely on classroom instruction.

---

## Our Goal

We aim to develop **10 high-quality SSCE textbooks by 2030**, starting with:

- Mathematics  
- Physics  
- Chemistry  
- Biology  
- Agricultural Science  

More subjects will be added as the initiative grows.

---

## Why CTRI Exists

Many students struggle with textbooks that are either too complex, too theoretical, or not structured for self-learning.

CTRI is designed to solve this by focusing on:

- Clear explanations  
- Step-by-step learning  
- Structured progression  
- Student-friendly language
